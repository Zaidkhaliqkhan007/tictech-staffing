IMPORTANT ABOUT SOUND
Modern Chrome, Safari, Edge, iPhone, and Android browsers do not allow websites to start music automatically before user interaction. V5.1 follows that browser rule. Visitors use the Sound control in the header. After enabled, the generative ambient score responds to scrolling and section changes.
